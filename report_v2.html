4 · Logistics & lessons

Planning each ceremony as its own deployment

ChallengeHow we managed it
DistanceChose vehicle rental or ground transport per district and scheduled travel to arrive in time.
Security & accessPlanned movements with the project team and local partners; kept travel within safe hours.
Carrying fridgesHelped each family carry their fridge so it reached them safely.
ConsistencySame service package and checklist in all 8 districts.

What worked well

  • A standard service package for every district.
  • Planning each ceremony as its own deployment.
  • Helping families carry their fridges on the day.
  • Regular contact with the project focal point.

For future handovers

  • Share a district schedule with the PIU early.
  • Confirm transport mode and venue per district in advance.
  • Agree handover arrangements with the focal point before the first ceremony.
