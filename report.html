Executive Summary

Rasmi Logistic Service delivered the media services and event management for the handover of solar fridges and milk storage containers to women milk vendors across eight districts in Somalia between 18 August and 30 September 2026. The ceremonies in the two South West districts are on hold.

The handover ceremonies formed part of the HoA DRIVE Project, managed by the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) of the Federal Government of Somalia’s Ministry of Finance, with support from the World Bank.

Section 2

Background & Scope of Services

The HoA DRIVE Project supports livelihoods in Somalia. Solar-powered fridges and milk storage containers give women milk vendors a way to keep milk fresh, reduce spoilage and sell with more confidence. Public handover ceremonies recognise the beneficiaries, show the project’s results to communities and partners, and create a visible record of the support provided. The programme covers ten districts; eight have been delivered and two are on hold.

Our four service lines

1

Media coverage

Professional coverage of the ceremony in each district, capturing the handover, the beneficiaries and the official proceedings for the project record.

2

Transport & logistics

Vehicle rental or ground transport for our teams and materials, matched to each district’s distance and access.

3

Catering

Refreshments for the ceremony, so that participants and guests were hosted comfortably throughout.

4

Equipment handling

Careful handling of 100 fridges and milk storage containers per district: unloading, positioning and staging for handover.

Section 4

Logistics & Coordination

Eight ceremonies in four regions meant eight separate field operations. We planned each district as its own deployment, with the same standards of service in all of them.

Vehicle rental

Used in six districts: Jowhar, Beledweyne, Kismayo, Dhusamareeb, Galkacyo and Mogadishu. The vehicle stayed with the team for the duration of the ceremony work.

Ground transport

Used in two districts: Balcad and Cadaado, where a transport arrangement for the day suited the location.

Coordination approach

  • One plan per district covering media, transport, catering and equipment handling.
  • Equipment handling planned around the 100-fridge allocation per district.
  • Regular contact with the PIU so that each ceremony matched the project’s expectations.

Section 6

Lessons Learned & Recommendations

What worked well

  • A standard service package for every district.
  • Planning each ceremony as its own deployment.
  • Pairing media coverage with equipment handling in one team.

For future handovers

  • Share a district schedule with the PIU early.
  • Confirm transport mode and venue per district in advance.
  • Agree the media outputs and deadlines with the client before the first ceremony.
