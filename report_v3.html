Section 2

Background & Scope of Services

Under the HoA DRIVE Project, solar-powered fridges are provided to women milk vendors so they can keep milk fresh, reduce spoilage and increase income from milk sales. Public handover ceremonies were organised in each target district to hand the fridges to beneficiary families and to create a visible record of the support provided.

The fridges were supplied by the project. Rasmi Logistic Service provided logistics support for the handover, as set out below.

Service 1 · All 9 districts

Fridge carrying and handling

Loading, moving and carrying the fridge equipment to the venue, and helping each of the 60 families per district carry their fridge after handover.

Service 2 · Afgoye and Balcad only

Catering

Refreshments for participants and guests at the ceremony.

Service 3 · All 9 districts

Vehicle rent and transportation

Vehicle rent within the city, and intercity transport where required, for the team, materials and fridges.

Service 4 · All 9 districts

Media coverage

Coverage of each ceremony, capturing the handover and the families receiving their fridges for the project record.

Section 4

Logistics & Coordination

Each district was planned and run as a separate deployment, with the same service standard applied at every site.

  • Transportation: vehicle rent within the city to move the team, materials and fridges between storage and the ceremony venue, and intercity transport where the ceremony site required travel from another city.
  • Catering: provided in Afgoye and Balcad only, in line with the contract. No catering was provided in the other seven districts.
  • Coordination: regular contact with the project focal point, Abdulkadir Hussein Hassan, before and during each ceremony to confirm dates, venues and arrangements.

Section 6

Lessons Learned & Recommendations

What worked well

  • A standard service checklist for every district.
  • Planning each ceremony as its own deployment.
  • Helping families carry their fridges on the day.

For future handovers

  • Agree the district schedule with the PIU early.
  • Confirm venue, transport type and any catering per district in advance.
  • Share media coverage with the PIU within a set number of days after each ceremony.
