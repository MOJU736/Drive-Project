Background & Scope of Services

The HoA DRIVE Project supports livelihoods in Somalia. Solar-powered fridges give women milk vendors a way to keep milk fresh, reduce spoilage and sell with more confidence. Public handover ceremonies recognise the beneficiaries and create a visible record of the support provided.

We won this project, and we delivered four services at every ceremony.

Service 1

Media coverage

Coverage of the ceremony in each district, capturing the handover and the families receiving their fridges for the project record.

Service 2

Fridges for 60 families

In each district, 60 families received their fridges, and our team helped them carry the fridges.

Service 3

Catering

Refreshments at each ceremony, so participants and guests were hosted comfortably.

Service 4

Transportation

Vehicle rental or ground transport, matched to each district’s distance and access.

Section 4

Logistics & Coordination

Eight ceremonies in four regions meant eight separate field operations. We planned each district as its own deployment, with the same standards of service in all of them.

Vehicle rental

Used in six districts: Jowhar, Beledweyne, Kismayo, Dhusamareeb, Galkacyo and Mogadishu.

Ground transport

Used in two districts: Balcad and Cadaado, where a transport arrangement for the day suited the location.

Coordination approach

  • One plan per district covering transport, catering, fridge handover and media coverage.
  • Support to families sized to the 60 fridges handed over in each district.
  • Regular contact with the project focal point, Abdulkadir Hussein Hassan, so that each ceremony matched the project’s expectations.

Section 6

Lessons Learned & Recommendations

What worked well

  • A standard service package for every district.
  • Planning each ceremony as its own deployment.
  • Helping families carry their fridges on the day.

For future handovers

  • Share a district schedule with the PIU early.
  • Confirm transport mode and venue per district in advance.
  • Agree handover arrangements with the focal point before the first ceremony.
